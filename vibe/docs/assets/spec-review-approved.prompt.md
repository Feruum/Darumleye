## Prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Original 16:9 desktop application design for a Russian-language FinTech requirements validator (SPEC REVIEW). Structural inspiration only: user-provided example screen with a narrow full-height sidebar, slim white header, light gray background, main title above two functional panels. Use our BCC Hub-inspired palette instead: rich purple sidebar #341A59, vibrant violet #7938E8 primary action, white panels, warm light gray canvas #F5F4F2, almost-black typography #19151E. Sidebar roughly 200 pixels wide, simple 'spec review' wordmark, small subline 'FINTECH / BCC HUB × MNU', compact navigation 'Обзор', 'Проверка ТЗ' selected in white pill, 'Настройки'. Top bar white, 54 pixels tall, title 'Проверка ТЗ'. Main content: small violet uppercase eyebrow 'РАБОЧАЯ ОБЛАСТЬ', large strong heading 'Проверка требований', small muted subtitle 'Проверьте ТЗ перед началом разработки.' Align one compact violet button 'Загрузить документ' at right. Below: two simple white panels with very subtle borders and modest 8px corners, left about 32% width with heading 'Документ', a compact dashed upload field 'PDF, TXT, MD' and button 'Проверить ТЗ'; right 68% width with heading 'Результаты проверки', a white mostly empty area, a small violet outline document icon and centered plain text 'Загрузите документ, чтобы начать проверку'. Keep it restrained, quiet, practical, polished, with lots of useful whitespace. No gradients, illustrations, marketing hero, excessive chips or metrics, no fabricated values, charts or decorative patterns. Do not reproduce the reference brand or its copy. Use realistic crisp Russian UI typography, design at normal app scale, not giant headline. No browser chrome, no watermark.
